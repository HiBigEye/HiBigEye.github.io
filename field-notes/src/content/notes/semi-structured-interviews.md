A semi-structured interview follows a **guide** of open questions but leaves room to follow up on whatever the participant brings up.

## Tips

- Start broad, then narrow down.
- Ask about concrete past experiences, not hypotheticals.
- Analyse the transcripts with **thematic analysis**: code → group codes → themes.
