Cognitive science studies the mind by bringing together psychology, neuroscience, linguistics, philosophy, anthropology and computer science.

## Marr's three levels

| Level | Question |
|---|---|
| Computational | *What* problem is being solved, and why? |
| Algorithmic | *How* — which representations and procedures? |
| Implementational | *Where* — how is it realised physically? |
