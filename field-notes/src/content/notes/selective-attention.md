The core puzzle: the senses deliver far more information than we can consciously process, so something has to **select**.

## Early vs late selection

- **Broadbent (1958)** — an *early* filter selects by physical features (pitch, location) before meaning is processed.
- **Treisman (1964)** — the filter *attenuates* rather than blocks: unattended input is weakened, not lost. That's why you still notice your own name at a party.
- **Deutsch & Deutsch (1963)** — *late* selection: everything is analysed for meaning, and selection happens just before response.

## Takeaway

Attention looks less like a gate and more like a **weighting** of inputs — an idea that returns, almost literally, in [attention in transformers](/notes/transformer-attention/).
