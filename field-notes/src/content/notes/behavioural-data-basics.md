Behavioural data records what people **do** — clicks, response times, movements, choices — rather than what they say they do.

## Watch out for

- **Selection bias**: who is (not) in the data?
- **Construct validity**: does the measure capture the concept?
- **Ethics**: consent and privacy come first.
