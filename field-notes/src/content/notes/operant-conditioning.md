Behaviour followed by a pleasant consequence becomes more likely (Thorndike's *law of effect*; Skinner's operant conditioning).

| | Add a stimulus | Remove a stimulus |
|---|---|---|
| **Behaviour increases** | Positive reinforcement | Negative reinforcement |
| **Behaviour decreases** | Positive punishment | Negative punishment |

The same logic — act, receive reward, adjust — is the backbone of reinforcement learning in AI.
